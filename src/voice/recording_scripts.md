# Voice Cloning Recording Scripts (Dad & Mom)

This guide provides phonetic Cantonese scripts designed to build high-fidelity voice clones in ElevenLabs (Multilingual v2) or open-source neural TTS models.

## Recording Best Practices

1. **Environment**: Quiet room with minimal echo (a carpeted bedroom or closet with hanging clothes works wonders).
2. **Microphone**: Keep the mic/phone 6–8 inches from your mouth at a 45-degree angle (to avoid plosive breath pops on *p, t, k* sounds).
3. **Tone (Parentese / 媽媽語 / 爸爸語)**: Speak with gentle, animated warmth—the exact comforting, cheerful voice you use when playing with the twins. Avoid flat or monotone corporate speaking.
4. **Speed**: Slightly slower and clearer than adult conversation, with gentle musical cadence.
5. **Length**: 1.5 to 2 minutes of continuous clean speech is ideal.
6. **File Format**: Save as `.wav` or high-quality `.mp3` (44.1kHz or 48kHz, mono or stereo).

## 🎙️ Dad's Voice Recording Script (粵語 Cantonese)

> **Instructions for Dad**: Read through the following paragraphs naturally. Feel free to smile while speaking—smiling naturally brightens vocal timbre!

### Section 1: Morning & Greeting (早晨同問候)
- **Cantonese**: 兩個小寶貝，早晨呀！訓醒未呀？爸爸喺度呀！今日出面天氣好靚喎，有太陽，等爸爸抱抱你哋啦！哥哥伸個懶腰，細佬又笑得咁開心！一齊嚟梳洗換靚衫啦！
- **Jyutping**: *Loeng5 go3 siu2 bou2 bui3, zou2 san4 aa3! Fan3 seng2 mei6 aa3? Baa4-baa1 hai2 dou6 aa3! Gam1 jat6 ceot1 min6 tin1 hei3 hou2 leng3 wo3, jau5 taai3 joeng4, dang2 baa4-baa1 pou5-pou5 nei5 dei6 laa1! Go4-go1 san1 go3 laan5 jiu1, sai3-lou2 jau6 siu3 dak1 gam3 hoi1 sam1! Jat1 cai4 lai4 so1 sai2 wun6 leng3 saam1 laa1!*
- **English**: Two little treasures, good morning! Are you awake yet? Dad is right here! The weather outside is so nice today, sunny, let Dad give you both a big hug! Big brother is stretching, and little brother is smiling so happily! Let's get washed up and change into nice clothes together!

### Section 2: Breakfast & Routine (食早餐同日常)
- **Cantonese**: 肚餓喇可？爸爸準備咗香噴噴嘅早餐喎！有熱辣辣嘅燕麥粥，仲有甜甜嘅香蕉片同藍莓。嚟，開大個口，「啊——」真係好乖！食飽飽就有力氣去玩波波同睇書書喇！
- **Jyutping**: *Tou5 ngo6 laa3 ho2? Baa4-baa1 zeon2 bei6 zo2 hoeng1 pan1 pan1 ge3 zou2 caan1 wo3! Jau5 jit6 laat6 laat6 ge3 jin3 mak6 zuk1, zung6 jau5 tim4 tim4 ge3 hoeng1 ziu1 pin2 tung4 laam4 mei4. Lai4, hoi1 daai6 go3 hau2, "Aa——" zan1 hai6 hou2 gwaai1! Sik6 baau2 baau2 zau6 jau5 lik6 hei3 heoi3 waan2 bo1-bo1 tung4 tai2 syu1-syu1 laa3!*
- **English**: Getting hungry, right? Dad made delicious, fragrant breakfast! Warm oatmeal, plus sweet banana slices and blueberries. Come on, open wide, "Ahhh—" what good boys! Once you're full, you'll have lots of energy to play with the balls and read books!

### Section 3: Playtime & Family Love (一齊玩同錫錫)
- **Cantonese**: 聽下，係狗狗汪汪叫喎！狗狗都想同你哋玩拋波波。陣間公公婆婆、爺爺嫲嫲、姑媽同表哥都會嚟搵我哋玩㗎。爸爸好愛你哋，哥哥同細佬都要相親相愛，做個快樂嘅好寶寶！
- **Jyutping**: *Teng1 haa5, hai6 gau2-gau2 wong1-wong1 giu3 wo3! Gau2-gau2 dou1 soeng2 tung4 nei5 dei6 waan2 paau1 bo1-bo1. Zan6 gaan1 gung1-gung1 po4-po4, je4-je4 maa4-maa4, gu1-maa1 tung4 biu2-go1 dou1 wui5 lai4 wan2 ngo5 dei6 waan2 gaa3. Baa4-baa1 hou2 oi3 nei5 dei6, go4-go1 tung4 sai3-lou2 dou1 jiu3 soeng1 can1 soeng1 oi3, zou6 go3 faai3 lok6 ge3 hou2 bou2-bou2!*
- **English**: Listen, that's the dog barking "woof woof"! The dog wants to play fetch with you too. In a little bit, Grandpa, Grandma, Auntie, and Cousin will all come play with us. Dad loves you both so much. Big brother and little brother must always love each other and be happy, wonderful babies!

## 🎙️ Mom's Voice Recording Script (粵語 Cantonese)

> **Instructions for Mom**: Read with a soft, melodious, comforting voice.

### Section 1: Gentle Hugs & Reading (溫柔抱抱同讀書)
- **Cantonese**: 媽媽嘅得意小寶貝，快啲過嚟媽媽懷抱啦！錫一啖，呖！今日媽媽同你哋讀一本好得意嘅圖畫書，睇下呢度有隻黃色小鴨，「呷呷呷」，仲有架紅色嘅消防車，「嗚嗚嗚」咁叫喎！
- **Jyutping**: *Maa4-maa1 ge3 dak1 ji3 siu2 bou2 bui3, faai3 di1 gwo3 lai4 maa4-maa1 waai4 pou5 laa1! Sek3 jat1 daam6, le1! Gam1 jat6 maa4-maa1 tung4 nei5 dei6 duk6 jat1 bun2 hou2 dak1 ji3 ge3 tou4 waa2 syu1, tai2 haa5 nei1 dou6 jau5 zek3 wong4 sik1 siu2 aap3, "ngap1 ngap1 ngap1", zung6 jau5 gaa3 hung4 sik1 ge3 siu1 fong4 ce1, "wu1 wu1 wu1" gam3 giu3 wo3!*
- **English**: Mom's sweet little treasures, come quickly into Mom's arms! Give you a kiss, mwah! Today Mom is going to read a delightful picture book with you. Look here, there's a little yellow duck, "quack quack quack", and a big red fire truck going "wee-woo wee-woo"!

### Section 2: Park Walk & Exploration (公園散步同探索)
- **Cantonese**: 媽媽推架雙人BB車，帶你哋去公園散步囉！睇下綠色嘅樹葉，仲有彩色嘅蝴蝶飛嚟飛去。細佬你睇下嗰邊，哥哥都指住朵大紅花笑緊喎！大自然真係好得意呀！
- **Jyutping**: *Maa4-maa1 teoi1 gaa3 soeng1 jan4 bi4-bi1 ce1, daai3 nei5 dei6 heoi3 gung1 jyun2 saan2 bou6 lo3! Tai2 haa5 luk6 sik1 ge3 syu6 jip6, zung6 jau5 coi2 sik1 ge3 wu4 dip2 fei1 lai4 fei1 heoi3. Sai3-lou2 nei5 tai2 haa5 go2 bin1, go4-go1 dou1 zi2 zyu6 do2 daai6 hung4 faa1 siu3 gan2 wo3! Daai6 zi6 jin4 zan1 hai6 hou2 dak1 ji3 aa3!*
- **English**: Mom is pushing the twin stroller to take you for a walk in the park! Look at the green tree leaves, and the colorful butterflies fluttering around. Little brother look over there, big brother is pointing at a big red flower and smiling! Nature is so wonderful!

### Section 3: Lullaby & Bedtime (晚安與搖籃曲)
- **Cantonese**: 天黑黑喇，月亮星星出嚟喇。沖個暖笠笠嘅泡泡浴，換件軟綿綿嘅睡衣。媽媽唱首歌仔俾你哋聽：月光光，照地堂，蝦仔你乖乖訓落床。兩個BB乖乖訓覺，聽朝再玩過啦，晚安！
- **Jyutping**: *Tin1 hak1 hak1 laa3, jyut6 loeng6 seng1 seng1 ceot1 lai4 laa3. Cung1 go3 nyun5 lap1 lap1 ge3 pou5 pou5 juk6, wun6 gin6 jyun5 min4 min4 ge3 seoi6 ji1. Maa4-maa1 coeng3 sau2 go1 zai2 bei2 nei5 dei6 teng1: jyut6 gwong1 gwong1, ziu3 dei6 tong4, haa1 zai2 nei5 gwaai1 gwaai1 fan3 lok6 cong4. Loeng5 go3 bi4-bi1 gwaai1 gwaai1 fan3 gaau3, ting1 ziu1 zoi3 waan2 gwo3 laa1, maan5 on1!*
- **English**: It's getting dark, the moon and stars have come out. Time for a warm bubble bath and cozy soft pajamas. Mom will sing a lullaby for you: "Bright moon, shining on the ground, little boy sleep sweetly in bed." Sleep tightly, sweet babies, we'll play again tomorrow morning, goodnight!
